Service

Revenue Cycle Consulting

Practical revenue cycle guidance for independent and growing practices, from front desk to final payment.

Where we help

  • Workflow mapping from scheduling and eligibility to charge entry and payment posting
  • Charge capture and missed-charge review
  • Coding and documentation optimization
  • Key performance indicators: clean claim rate, denial rate, days in A/R and net collection rate
  • Payer mix and reimbursement trend reviews
  • Billing vendor or in-house team performance assessments

How an engagement works

We start with a short discovery assessment, share findings and a prioritized plan, then support implementation for as long as you need. Engagements can be one-time or ongoing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you replace our billing company?

No. We work alongside your in-house team or billing vendor to improve results and give you independent visibility.

Which KPIs should a practice track?

At minimum: clean claim rate, denial rate, days in accounts receivable and net collection rate, reviewed monthly.
